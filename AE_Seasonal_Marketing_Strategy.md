# American Eagle — Seasonal Marketing Strategy Guide
### Back-to-School & Holiday Campaign Playbook

**Prepared for:** American Eagle Marketing Team  
**Data period:** FY2021 (52-week analysis, Jan 2021 – Jan 2022)  
**Objective:** Maximize revenue during the two peak windows identified in the sales trend analysis

---

## Executive Summary

Sales data reveals two high-velocity windows that together define the brand's revenue ceiling:

| Period | Weeks | Peak Weekly Revenue | vs. Annual Avg ($183M) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Back-to-School | Wks 29–32 (Aug 14 – Sep 4) | $242M | +32% |
| Holiday Surge | Wks 45–52 (Dec 4 – Jan 22) | $264M | +44% |

The 20-week plateau between these peaks (Wks 33–44, avg $167M) represents the single largest untapped revenue opportunity. This playbook addresses both how to amplify the peaks and how to bridge the valley.

---

## Part 1: Back-to-School Campaign Strategy
### Target window: July 15 – September 10 (Weeks 27–33)

Back-to-school is AE's heritage moment. Teens and young adults refreshing their wardrobes for the school year are the brand's core buyer — this is the highest-intent shopping period of the first three quarters.

---

### Step 1 — Pre-season audience warm-up (6 weeks before peak)
**Timeline: July 1 – July 28**

1. **Reactivate the loyalty database.** Pull all customers who purchased during last year's BTS window and segment them into three tiers: high-spenders ($200+), mid-tier ($75–199), and lapsed (no purchase in 90+ days). Each tier gets a tailored reactivation sequence via email and push notification.

2. **Launch a teaser campaign on TikTok and Instagram Reels.** Release 3–5 short-form videos (15–30 sec) showcasing the new fall denim and tops collection. Do not reveal prices yet — focus on style and aspiration. Use trending audio and challenge formats to maximize organic reach.

3. **Seed micro-influencers.** Partner with 20–40 college and high school-age micro-influencers (50K–500K followers) in major metro markets (New York, LA, Chicago, Toronto). Send them early access to the BTS collection 4 weeks before the campaign launches so content goes live organically during the teaser window. Prioritize authenticity over polish.

4. **Build the email countdown sequence.** Schedule a 4-email pre-launch sequence:
   - Email 1 (5 weeks out): "Something's coming — new denim drops soon"
   - Email 2 (3 weeks out): First look at 3–5 hero pieces
   - Email 3 (1 week out): "BTS event starts in 7 days" with loyalty early access offer
   - Email 4 (launch day): Full collection reveal with CTA

---

### Step 2 — Launch week execution (peak activation)
**Timeline: August 8 – August 21**

1. **Drop the BTS collection in a single launch event.** Designate one day (recommend a Thursday) as the official "AE BTS Drop Day." Release the full collection online at 9 AM Eastern with simultaneous in-store activation. Create urgency with a 48-hour launch promotion (e.g., free shipping + 15% off first BTS purchase for loyalty members).

2. **Run a "Build Your Look" interactive campaign.** Deploy a digital outfit builder on the AE website and app that lets shoppers assemble a complete back-to-school wardrobe — jeans, tops, and accessories — and purchase the full look with one click. This increases average order value (AOV) by reducing decision fatigue and cross-selling into Aerie and Accessories, the two highest-margin categories.

3. **Activate paid media at full spend.** This is the highest-ROI window of Q2/Q3. Allocate paid budget across:
   - Meta (Instagram + Facebook): Retargeting warm audiences from teaser campaign
   - TikTok: Broad reach on 16–24 demographic
   - Google Shopping: Capture high-intent search traffic ("back to school outfits," "AE jeans")
   - Snapchat: Reach high school audience specifically

4. **Deploy in-store experiential activations.** In top-50 performing store locations, create a dedicated "BTS zone" with curated outfit displays, fitting room fast-pass lanes for BTS shoppers, and a photo moment (branded backdrop) tied to the social campaign hashtag. Train associates to recommend complete looks rather than individual items.

5. **Run a loyalty double-points event.** For AE Real Rewards members, offer 2x or 3x points on all BTS purchases during the first week of the campaign. This drives both loyalty sign-ups and repeat purchasing without offering a blanket discount.

---

### Step 3 — Peak sustain and tail capture (post-launch)
**Timeline: August 22 – September 10**

1. **Introduce a "Last chance" messaging phase.** In Week 3 of the BTS window, shift messaging from new arrivals to scarcity: "These styles are going fast" and "Complete your look before sizes sell out." This maintains purchase urgency as the organic peak begins to fade.

2. **Target late-decision buyers with paid retargeting.** Shoppers who visited the BTS collection pages but did not convert during launch week are the highest-priority retargeting segment. Serve them dynamic product ads showing exactly the items they viewed, with a limited-time free shipping offer.

3. **Launch a "Dorm Room Essentials" sub-campaign.** Position Aerie in the final 2 weeks of BTS as a must-have dorm and comfort layer — loungewear, intimates, and activewear framed for the college-move-in moment. This extends BTS relevance beyond just outfits and pulls Aerie revenue forward from its typical seasonal baseline.

## Part 2: Holiday Campaign Strategy
### Target window: November 1 – January 22 (Weeks 43–52)

The holiday surge is the single most valuable sustained revenue window in the year. Unlike BTS which spikes and drops sharply, holiday revenue holds above $246M for 8 consecutive weeks — meaning the opportunity is not just to peak higher but to extend the elevated run as long as possible.

---

### Step 1 — Pre-holiday brand positioning (4 weeks before peak)
**Timeline: November 1 – November 26**

1. **Shift brand tone to gifting.** Beginning November 1, reposition all creative, email, and social content around gifting. AE is not just a self-purchase brand during this window — it is a gift destination for parents buying for teens, partners shopping for each other, and young adults buying for friends. All messaging should acknowledge this dual buyer (the gift-giver and the recipient).

2. **Build and promote gift guides.** Publish three tiered gift guides on the website and distribute via email and social:
   - "Under $50" (Accessories, basics, Aerie essentials)
   - "Under $100" (Denim, tops, activewear bundles)
   - "The Full Look" ($100+, complete outfit gifting)
   
   Gift guides serve double duty: they increase AOV by showing curated bundles and they capture SEO traffic from gift-intent searches during November.

3. **Launch an Aerie gifting push.** Aerie's Intimates and Accessories carry 44–45% gross margins — the highest in the portfolio. Build a dedicated Aerie holiday gifting collection (gift sets, body care bundles, cozy-season kits) and promote it through AE channels. This is the highest-margin revenue available during the peak window.

4. **Set up gift card infrastructure.** Ensure gift cards are prominently featured across all channels — email, homepage, paid ads, and in-store. Data shows revenue holds elevated through January 22, which is directly attributable to gift card redemptions. Maximizing gift card sales during December directly extends the high-revenue window into January.

---

### Step 2 — Black Friday / Cyber Monday execution
**Timeline: November 27 – December 2**

1. **Do not lead with deep discounts.** AE's gross margin averages 40–45%. Blanket 40–50% discount events destroy margin without building loyalty. Instead, structure the BFCM offer as:
   - Loyalty members: Early access (Wednesday before Black Friday) + 25% off sitewide
   - New customers: 20% off first purchase + free shipping
   - All shoppers: Bonus gift card ($10 bonus card for every $75 spent) to drive January return visits

2. **Run a 5-day "AE Holiday Event" rather than a single Black Friday sale.** Starting the Wednesday before Thanksgiving through Cyber Monday, release one featured deal or new drop per day to sustain daily traffic and repeat visits. Each day's offer should be limited in quantity or time to prevent shoppers from waiting.

3. **Maximize Cyber Monday digital investment.** Cyber Monday is the highest-traffic online shopping day of the year. Ensure:
   - Website load testing is completed in advance (page speed directly affects conversion rate)
   - All paid media budgets are front-loaded to Monday
   - Abandoned cart sequences are reduced from 24-hour to 4-hour triggers during BFCM week

---

### Step 3 — Peak holiday sustain (December)
**Timeline: December 4 – December 24**

1. **Run a weekly "New Arrivals" drop cadence.** Release small batches of new holiday product every Thursday in December. This gives returning customers a reason to check back weekly and keeps email open rates elevated throughout the month rather than cannibalizing attention into a single send.

2. **Implement shipping deadline urgency messaging.** The most powerful conversion trigger in December is the guaranteed delivery deadline. Build a countdown to the last standard shipping date and the last express shipping date prominently into the homepage, email headers, and all paid ad creative starting December 12.

3. **Activate store associates as styling consultants.** December in-store traffic is driven by gift-givers who are less familiar with the brand. Train associates to lead with "Who are you shopping for?" rather than product features. Script three complete outfit recommendations by age and gender (teen girl, teen boy, young adult) that associates can execute in under 3 minutes. This lifts AOV on in-store gifting transactions.

4. **Deploy SMS marketing for last-minute shoppers.** Reserve SMS sends (limited to 2–3 per month under best practice guidelines) for the two highest-impact moments: the last free standard shipping day, and the last in-store pickup deadline (typically December 23). SMS open rates for time-sensitive messages exceed 90% — this is the highest-converting channel for deadline-driven conversion.

---

### Step 4 — January extension (the overlooked goldmine)
**Timeline: December 25 – January 22**

The data shows revenue remains above $246M through all four weeks of January — this is gift card redemption season and it is systematically under-marketed by most retailers.

1. **Launch a "New Year, New Wardrobe" campaign on December 26.** The day after Christmas is the single highest gift card redemption day of the year. Have a fully built campaign ready to go live — do not wait until January. Email all gift card purchasers (from the previous 30 days) with redemption inspiration content and styling suggestions.

2. **Run a "Treat Yourself" positioning in early January.** Shift messaging from gifting back to self-purchase. January is resolution season — frame AE as the wardrobe upgrade that makes the new year feel fresh. Activewear and Aerie collections are especially well-suited to this positioning.

3. **Extend loyalty bonus points through January 15.** To sustain the elevated revenue tail, run a loyalty double-points event during the first two weeks of January. This is the lowest-cost way to drive repeat purchasing from the large cohort of holiday first-time buyers and gift card redeemers.

4. **Capture post-holiday exchange traffic.** Exchanges and returns peak December 26 – January 8. Train associates and configure the app and website to present upgrade and upsell options at the point of return/exchange. A customer returning a $60 item is a warm audience for a $90 item — staff should be scripted for this conversion moment.

## Part 3: Bridging the Revenue Valley
### Target window: September 11 – October 31 (Weeks 33–44, avg $167M)

This 12-week stretch between peaks averages $16M below the annual baseline. Closing even half that gap adds approximately $100M+ in annual revenue.

1. **Schedule a fall capsule drop in early October.** A limited-edition fall collection released in the first week of October gives the brand a reason to send high-urgency communications during the quiet window. Price the capsule at a slight premium to standard collection items to protect margin.

2. **Run a "Refer a Friend" loyalty campaign in September.** After the BTS peak, the brand has its largest fresh customer cohort of the year. A referral campaign launched in September (when BTS excitement is still fresh) converts new BTS buyers into brand advocates. Offer both parties a reward (e.g., $15 off next purchase for referrer and referee).

3. **Test Canada as a promotional laboratory.** With ~27% of revenue and a contained market footprint, the Canadian segment is the ideal environment to pilot new promotional mechanics — flash sales, bundle pricing, dynamic discounting — before rolling them out to the US at full scale during the holiday window.
